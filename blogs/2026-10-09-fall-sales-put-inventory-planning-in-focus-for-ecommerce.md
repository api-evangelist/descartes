Fall Sales Put Inventory Planning in Focus for Ecommerce Sellers Amazon, Target, and Walmart are holding overlapping October sales, giving ecommerce sellers an opportunity to assess demand before the holiday season. According to a Modern Retail article , Amazon’s Prime Big Deal Days and Target’s Circle sale are scheduled for October 6–7, 2026. Walmart’s Fall Deals Sale runs October 5–11.
